## 🤸 What is Finger-Frame-AI?

Finger-Frame-AI is a revolutionary **video transformation tool** that lets you use your **fingers as a frame** to create stunning, cinematic videos. It uses advanced artificial intelligence to track your hand movements in real-time and applies amazing video effects through that frame. Imagine pointing at a video and creating a beautiful portal, transforming the scene, or adding cool visual twists – all with just your hand gestures! This is a powerful yet easy-to-use application for Windows that brings the future of video editing to your fingertips.

**It's designed for everyday users** – you don't need to learn complex video editing software. Finger-Frame-AI does the heavy lifting for you.

## ✨ Key Features (What Makes it Awesome)

- 💪 **Stabilized Hand Tracking:** The AI perfectly follows your finger movements, even with fast motion, so your video frames stay smooth and steady.
- 🌌 **Perspective Portals:** Create a world inside a frame – the tool can transform the area inside your fingers into a completely different scene or use it for a direct view into another world.
- 🎨 **Reference-Guided Video Restyling:** Have a photo or image you like? Upload it, and the app can restyle your video to match that color tone or art style, applying a unified, cinematic look to everything.
- 👁️ **Occlusion Effects:** The app can see what’s in front of your fingers and behind them, allowing your video frame to act as a window into a scene, even if there are objects between you and the camera.
- ✨ **Cinematic Portal Transitions:** Smoothly morph your video between different scenes, using your hand as a transition element – it looks like a professional movie effect.

### 👩‍💻 Using Finger-Frame-AI (Your First Project)

Once you’ve launched the program, here’s your basic routine:

1.  **Webcam Check:** A window should open that starts with your webcam feed. Make sure your hand is visible to the camera.
2.  **Select a Source Video:** Click the **"Select Video"** button to choose a video file from your computer (MP4 or MOV work well).
3.  **Set Your Frame:** Use your index finger and thumb to make a "frame" shape (like a square) in front of your webcam. You’ll see a highlighted, transparent shape appear in real-time.
4.  **Choose a Mode:** Look for the “Effect mode” dropdown menu. Select one of the options:
    - **Portal:** Create a portal in the frame to another scene (you can choose a different video for the background).
    - **Restyle:** Upload the reference image you want to transfer.
    - **Basic:** Shows the video inside your hand frame.
5.  **Watch the Magic:** The AI will process the video inside your frame – your hand frame is the window into the effect. The app will run in near real-time, and you'll see the transformation live.
6.  **Export**: The output video will be automatically saved in the same folder as the original or you can use the **"Export"** button to save it to a specific location.

## 🛠️ Troubleshooting (Common Issues)

- **“Hand not detected”**: Ensure you are in a well-lit room. Move your hand within the camera's view. If it’s too far, step closer.
- **Lag in Portal:** If the video is laggy, try lowering the video resolution, or closing other programs.
- **Not working?** Check if your webcam is being used by another app (like Zoom or Teams). Close them.
- **Video not loading:** Use common formats (MP4, MOV, AVI). Convert if needed.
